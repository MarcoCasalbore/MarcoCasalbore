I'm a **PhD candidate in ICT at Sapienza University of Rome**, in the Department of Information Engineering, Electronics and Telecommunications (DIET).

My research focuses on **machine learning and quantum computing**. I also collaborate with industry on IoT, networking, and AI for networks.

### Research

- **Quantum machine learning:** hybrid quantum-classical methods for time-series anomaly detection.
- **IoT and networks:** LoRaWAN telemetry, device diagnostics, and downlink gateway selection.
- **Graph learning and digital twins:** monitoring and management of water distribution networks.
